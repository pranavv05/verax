# `public/images/`

General marketing imagery for the Verax website — as opposed to `public/product/`,
which is real screenshots of the app itself. Nothing is populated yet; this file
documents the intended contents of each subfolder so assets land in the right
place when they're sourced/commissioned.

Per the V1 design direction: no stock-photo aesthetic, no AI-generated
illustrations, no generic SaaS iconography. Every image here should look like
it was taken at, or made for, an actual Indian CA firm.

## `documents/`
Real photographs or high-fidelity scans of the paper Verax processes: GST
invoices, bank statements, bills, GSTR-2B extracts. This is the "real
accounting/document imagery" the brand direction calls for — used where the
hand-built mockups in `src/components/marketing/mockups/` aren't enough on
their own (e.g. a hero backdrop, an editorial section divider). Redact or
fabricate any figures/GSTINs before use — nothing here should be a real
client's document.

## `photography/`
Real human and workplace photography — a CA firm's office, hands on paperwork,
a team reviewing documents. MindMarket-style "human/real imagery," not stock
photography with generic office-worker energy and not AI-generated people.
Warm, working, unstaged over polished and posed.

## `editorial/`
Supporting abstract imagery used sparingly as section accents or texture —
paper grain, print/ink detail, subtle backgrounds. Not illustrations, not
icons, not decoration for its own sake — each one should earn a specific
section, the way MindMarket and Paraform use texture as punctuation rather
than wallpaper.
